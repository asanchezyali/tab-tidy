# Tab Tidy privacy policy

_Last updated: October 20, 2026_

Tab Tidy does not collect, store, sell or send any personal data.

## What the extension reads

To find duplicate tabs and sort them, Tab Tidy reads the URL of your open tabs
through the browser's `tabs` permission. It compares those URLs on your own
device, at the moment a tab opens or when you press a button. URLs are never
saved, logged, or sent anywhere.

## What the extension stores

- **Your settings** (the checkboxes on the settings page), in
  `chrome.storage.sync`. If you are signed in to your browser with sync turned
  on, the browser syncs these settings between your devices. Tab Tidy never
  sees them leave the browser.
- **The ids of tabs opened in the last few seconds**, in
  `chrome.storage.session`. The browser holds these in memory and clears
  them when it closes. They are numbers, not URLs.

## Network access

Tab Tidy makes no network requests. It has no analytics, no ads, no
remote code, and no server.
